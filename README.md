# Pokémon Card Rarity, Age, and Market Value

**Edgar Soberon** · INFO 4617 Final Project

**Research question:** To what extent are Pokémon card rarity and age associated with their current market value? I will explore whether cards from older sets have higher prices and whether rarity explains price differences compared with cards in newer sets. Using Pokémon TCG API data (Scrydex and/or pokewallet.io), I plan to collect a one-time snapshot of roughly 300–500 English cards spanning 1999–2026, flatten nested JSON into a table with pandas, and compare median prices by rarity and release period with boxplots and scatterplots of age versus price. I expect rarer cards to sell for more than common cards, but I do not expect age alone to be the strongest predictor—value is more likely to reflect rarity and age together than age by itself.
